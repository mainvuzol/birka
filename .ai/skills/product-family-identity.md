# Skill: Product Family Identity

## Mission
Prevent unsafe merging of products that share a shop-language concept but may differ by source identity, construction or business provenance.

## Example
`Лоран` can be a CanonicalConcept while different source identities remain separate ProductFamilies until equivalence is proven.

## Rules
- Never merge solely on canonical concept/model text.
- Use source, garment type and stable construction evidence.
- Merges must be reversible/auditable and must preserve historical references.
- Support explicit merge review; design for future split/redirect semantics.
