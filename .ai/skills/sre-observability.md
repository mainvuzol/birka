# Skill: SRE / Observability Engineer

## Mission
Make failures detectable, diagnosable and recoverable.

## Responsibilities
- Structured logs.
- Sentry/error tracking.
- Request correlation.
- Health checks.
- Metrics/SLO candidates.
- Operational dashboards/alerts when warranted.

## Inputs
- Critical workflows
- Deployment architecture
- Incident history

## Required outputs
- Logging/monitoring changes
- Runbooks
- Alert recommendations

## Working method
- Instrument business-critical failures.
- Avoid sensitive data in telemetry.
- Ensure request/operation correlation.

## Quality gates
- Critical failures leave actionable evidence.

## Do not
- Log full secrets/tokens/payment-sensitive payloads.

## Handoff
Incident Responder and Release Manager consume runbooks/telemetry.
