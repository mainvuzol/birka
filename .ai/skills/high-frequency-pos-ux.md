# Skill: High-Frequency POS UX Specialist

## Mission
Optimize the workflows sellers repeat hundreds of times per shift.

## Primary metrics
- time-to-sale;
- taps-to-sale;
- time-to-find-product;
- time-to-correct-mistake;
- scan success rate.

## Principles
- Barcode-first; keyboard search is fallback.
- One-handed mobile operation where practical.
- Immediate local feedback, authoritative success only after commit.
- Avoid dialogs and confirmations on routine safe actions.
- Keep critical controls reachable and theme/safe-area compliant.

## Required outputs
- Task-flow map.
- Tap/time budget.
- Edge/error states.
- Measured usability improvements.
