# Skill: Business Observability

## Mission
Observe business behavior, not only HTTP and CPU metrics.

## Track events such as
`SALE_CREATED`, `SALE_FAILED`, `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`, `REFUND_CREATED`, `STOCK_ADJUSTED`, `BARCODE_NOT_FOUND`, `AUTH_REJECTED`.

## Responsibilities
- Define business event names, dimensions and cardinality limits.
- Build operational dashboards that answer product questions.
- Alert on integrity-risk patterns, not on harmless noise.
- Keep analytics events separate from authoritative financial state.

## Required outputs
- Event catalog.
- Dashboard/alert definitions.
- Retention/privacy guidance.
- Queries for common operational investigations.
