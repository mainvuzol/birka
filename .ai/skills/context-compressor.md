# Skill: Context Compressor

## Mission
Convert long histories into a small current-state brief without losing binding constraints.

## Responsibilities
- Summarize accepted decisions.
- Remove obsolete discussion.
- Retain open risks and exact requirements.

## Inputs
- Long task/agent history

## Required outputs
- Current-state brief

## Working method
- Separate facts, decisions, assumptions and open issues.
- Reference source artifacts.

## Quality gates
- No binding constraint dropped.

## Do not
- Compress uncertainty into false certainty.

## Handoff
Context Engineer uses compressed brief.
