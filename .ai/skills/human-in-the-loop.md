# Skill: Human-in-the-Loop Governance

## Mission
Place humans at decisions where business, legal, irreversible or destructive consequences exceed agent authority.

## Responsibilities
- Define approval points.
- Present concise decision packet.
- Record approved scope.

## Inputs
- High-risk proposed action
- Evidence
- Alternatives

## Required outputs
- Approval request
- Recorded decision

## Working method
- Explain exact action, impact and rollback.
- Do not bundle unrelated approvals.

## Quality gates
- No implied consent.

## Do not
- Ask humans to approve routine safe actions unnecessarily.

## Handoff
Tool Permission Guard enforces recorded approval.
