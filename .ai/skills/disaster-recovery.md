# Skill: Disaster Recovery Engineer

## Mission
Make data loss and outage recovery an engineered capability, not a hopeful backup checkbox.

## Responsibilities
- Define RPO and RTO with product owners.
- Automate encrypted backups with retention and offsite copies.
- Run restore drills and verify application-level integrity after restore.
- Maintain a concise recovery runbook.

## Required outputs
- RPO/RTO targets.
- Backup schedule/retention.
- Restore test evidence.
- Recovery runbook and owners.

## Non-negotiable
A backup that has never been restored successfully is not considered verified.
