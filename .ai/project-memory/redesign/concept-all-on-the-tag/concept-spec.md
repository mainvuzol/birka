## 0. How this concept was synthesized (read once)

Three concepts were judged through three lenses (owner adoption; mobile/Telegram feasibility; brand and accessibility).
Totals: A «Пульс» 231, C «Усе на бірці» 228, B «Нитка» 209. Two lenses chose **A as the base** for its information
architecture and home; the brand lens chose **C's visual system**. The final concept is therefore:

- **Skeleton from A:** owner tabs «Сьогодні · Продаж · Замовлення · Гроші · Ще»; a seller counter mode with four tabs that
  opens on the till; a home that answers "how are we doing" (per-store columns, never-summed debts, data age on every
  figure) *and* "what needs me" (a ranked list of tags that close themselves); the silent morning bot message; first run as
  tags, not a wizard.
- **Skin from C:** greyboard + ink neutrals with one acid-lime *thread* accent and the flip rule; hang tags with an eyelet;
  size cells as the headline; the string of knots for order places; printed rectangular stickers; the tear-off stub.
- **Grafts from B:** the "blank line" capture (a pasted DM, a TTN or a notebook debt line, parsed on the phone, every chip
  confirmed); wants on a model + size that fire when goods arrive; the post-office day counter from the recorded
  «Прибув на пошту» date; the eyelet's fate states; packing grouped by warehouse; a calm end-of-day tie-off.
- **Grafts from C:** places as the language of goods and orders; held units visible on the size cell and never sellable;
  the model grid with reserve, «Брак», "in parcels, off the books" and a «?» column; «Відкласти під замовлення» from a
  model; "her sizes" read from the goods on her debts; capture text that never leaves the phone; kopecks always on drawer
  screens; the "drawer not closed yesterday" tag.
- **Fixes applied:** a reconciling line under the home's total; count reminders as plain lines (no checkboxes); the
  hero tag never depends on a customer link (default verb «Скопіювати ТТН»); the owner's store choice presented as owner
  question [Q-1] with the door chips as fallback; child store's private warehouse «Свій» gated by a grant in that store;
  two-warehouse stores get separate chips per warehouse (no split cells, no per-line question); no server-side chat
  drafts; no lock on selling during the count; no transfers screen; retention lines state 1 year / 3 years exactly; the
  dark-theme "ink tag" stays dark; every colour code has a shape twin.
- **Dropped:** B's chat-log home, cycling pinned bar, universal NLP composer (shorthand, voice, OCR), retention footer and
  webfont body; C's "no money on Today until the count", per-store toggle on Today, split cells, condensed caps on pay
  buttons and lock during the count; A's dark cockpit block, top-right «+», server-side 24 h draft and Undo on money.
- **One finding that overrides a judge:** C's type family **Sofia Sans cannot be used.** Rendered in Chromium from the
  served Google Fonts files, its default Cyrillic is the *Bulgarian* form (г looks like "ƨ", д like "g", л like "ʌ",
  ґ like a hook), and `lang="uk"` does not switch it; only `lang="ru"` does. That is unacceptable for a Ukrainian UI. The
  replacement **Fira Sans Extra Condensed + Fira Sans** (one superfamily in two widths, same editorial idea) was verified
  on the served files: standard Ukrainian forms, `tnum` present, **₴ U+20B4 present** (which also solves C's missing-₴
  flaw), і ї є ґ ’ № « » − present; tabular digits measured equal-width in Chromium from the woff2 subsets.

## 1. Concept

### 1.1 Name
**ALL ON THE TAG** — «Усе на бірці». The product is called «Бірка», the hang tag on a coat. In this concept the whole
hybrid shop lives on tags: the day is a tag, every coat is a tag, everything that needs her is a tag, and every record is
a stub torn off a tag.

### 1.2 Thesis (one line)
*One look tells her how both shops are doing and what needs her; one tap clears it; and every coat, coin and promise sits
on a tag she can trust, torn off only when it is really recorded.*

### 1.3 The story in five sentences
1. Liudmyla runs two coat shops and sells in three chat apps, so her truth sits in her head, a notebook and a hundred
   threads, and none of them can tell her at 08:30 that a parcel starts costing storage tomorrow.
2. Birka opens inside Telegram with no password and lands her on **Today**: one day-tag with both shops side by side and
   a short list of tags, each holding one thing that needs her and one verb to clear it.
3. Between customers she pastes a customer's message into the **blank line**; the phone reads the model and size, she
   taps to confirm, and the coat is held for that parcel so nobody can sell it at the counter.
4. Her sellers never see her cockpit: the till opens straight away, a sale is three taps, and the pay button *is* the
   way of paying.
5. Every write shows its fate on a stub — attached while the phone waits, torn when recorded once, knotted when settled —
   so at 20:06 the drawer is counted blind, the day is tied off, and the notebook stays in the drawer.

### 1.4 The ONE signature element: the tear-off stub «Корінець»
Real inventory tags have a perforated stub you tear off when the coat sells. In Birka **every write to the server is a
stub on a tag**, and its eyelet tells the record's fate in shape, not only colour:

| State | Shape | Means | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kept** «Збережено на телефоні» | stub still **attached** by a dashed perforation; eyelet = **dashed ring** | the press was sent, the answer was lost; the phone re-checks every 20 s; it will not be recorded twice [EXISTS rule 37] | till, payment, return, order step, drawer close |
| **Recorded** «Записано» | stub **torn** (zig-zag top edge, 6 px notches at both ends); eyelet = **filled** in the record's colour | the server recorded it once | receipt, payment recorded, order step done, reserved |
| **Settled** «Зав’язано» | eyelet with a **knot** of thread (ring + small loop) | nothing is left to check: a drawer day closed, a debt paid off, an order collected | close result, customer timeline, Today's «Зроблено» |

The same eyelet (8 px on row tags, 12 px on hero tags) is the state dot of every Today tag (filled = needs you; ring =
waiting on someone else; knot = done), the app's **only spinner** (the ring rotates while the server answers) and the app
mark (a chamfered tag with an eyelet and a lime thread loop, also the @BotFather loading icon). Nothing else in the
interface uses a chamfered silhouette with a hole.

## 3. Design principles (each with the test every screen must pass)

| # | Principle | The test a screen must pass |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Two answers at a glance.** The owner sees *how we are doing* and *what needs me* before she scrolls. | On a 390×844 phone, Today's first viewport shows today's sold figure for each store, owed per store, and at least the first two tags. Under 700 px of stable viewport height the day-tag collapses to one line so the first tag stays visible. |
| P2 | **One job per screen, one primary action under the thumb.** | Exactly one primary control (Telegram MainButton *or* the in-app action dock), placed in the bottom third. No full-width primary at the top. Rare things are in a sheet, a detail screen or «Ще». Screen budget: title + one context line; one switch; one field; a list or card; one action zone; at most one line of explanation. |
| P3 | **Every write states its fate.** | Every server write ends on a stub in one of three shapes (kept / recorded / settled). No "try again" on a write. No spinner without the eyelet ring. |
| P4 | **Money where it physically is, never one made-up sum.** | No screen adds the drawer, the terminal, the account, the carrier, customers and suppliers into one figure. Customer debts appear per store only. Every money figure carries its age («наживо» / «дані на 11:32»). Nobody sees what a drawer should hold before it is counted. |
| P5 | **The coat is the unit; sizes are the headline.** | Every goods row shows its sizes as cells with counts; held units are visible on the cell and cannot go into a counter basket; a coat's state is a *place* (shelf, basket, held, in a parcel, at the post office, collected, back, damaged). |
| P6 | **Nothing guessed, nothing kept that should not be.** | Every value read from a message is a chip with a dashed outline until a person taps it. An order shows no customer name, phone or address. Pasted text never leaves the phone. |
| P7 | **Rules invisible, counter fast.** | No screen says «Немає права»; missing rights mean missing items. A seller's sale is ≤ 3 taps and ≤ 15 s; nothing moves under the finger (lines appear in reserved room; skeletons keep places). |

[DEVIATION from DD §3, reason] DD's «Огляд · Продаж · Товар · Клієнти · Ще» becomes «Сьогодні · Продаж · Замовлення ·
Гроші · Ще». The online channel needs a home one tap away; money is the owner's first question; stock is answered by the
till's search, where every result *is* the model's stock by size; customers exist only through debts (BR:304-306), so they
are reached by search, from «Гроші», from the till's «Внесок» and from «Ще › Покупці». Sellers lose «Товар» and «Клієнти»
as tabs for the same reasons; debt payments move onto the till as the «Внесок» segment.

**Store choice (В6).** A seller or manager with one store never sees a store question. A person with 2+ stores who sells
gets the door question each opening (S06 drawn as the door variant). **The owner** lands on Today, because looking at the
network needs no store; the question «Де продаєте зараз?» appears as a sheet at her **first store-bound action** in this
opening (sell, return, payment, drawer, new order, goods arrived), is never remembered between openings, and «Змінити
магазин» sits in the context line of every store-bound screen. This is queued as owner question **Q-1**; the fallback, if
she says no, is the door question exactly as for sellers.

### 4.3 What is one tap away, what hides in a sheet
- **One tap from a tab root:** every tag's verb; the search on Today (models, customers, orders by № or TTN); the capture
  pill; the till's segment «Продати · Повернути · Внесок»; every money place on «Гроші»; each order's next step.
- **In a sheet:** the basket (qty, price, «Прибрати», «Оплата частинами»); the store question; the shortfall; the TTN entry;
  «Клієнт забрав — як оплатили?»; «Видати з каси»; the drawer-close confirmation; the reason for a difference; the capture
  sheet; «Пізніше» on a tag.
- **Pushed (Back appears):** model detail, order detail, customer card, debt, the count, the result, the sales report,
  closes, suppliers, catalogue, the receipt screens.
- **Telegram ⋯ › Settings** (`SettingsButton`): language EN / УКР; [PROPOSAL] morning message on/off, payment details for
  replies, free storage days per carrier (default 7, editable), Face ID before «Гроші», add to Home Screen.

### 4.4 Telegram native controls per screen
Global rules (none of the three concepts stated them; the build must):
1. Boot: render the skeleton; call `setHeaderColor(paper)`, `setBackgroundColor(paper)`, `setBottomBarColor(card)`
   **before** `ready()`; then `ready()`, `expand()`, `disableVerticalSwipes()`; lock nothing at boot.
2. `enableClosingConfirmation()` only while a basket has items, a count is being typed, or a capture draft is open; off
   otherwise.
3. `hideKeyboard()` on a size-cell tap, on a search-result tap and when an amount is confirmed. Every field sets
   `enterkeyhint` («search», «done», «next»). Layout height = `var(--tg-viewport-stable-height, 100dvh)`; never `100vh`.
4. **The till, returns, debt payment and the drawer close keep an in-app action dock** (the three ways of paying are
   three in-app buttons, never the MainButton, rules 3 and 41). The **MainButton** is reserved for screen-level single
   actions; it is set on screen entry and cleared on exit, and it is **never shown or hidden inside the sale loop**
   (each toggle resizes the viewport and moves content under the finger, rule 45). Enable/disable is allowed.
5. «Новий продаж», «Скопіювати ID», «Скопіювати відповідь», «Скопіювати ТТН» stay **in-app buttons**: `focus()` and
   clipboard writes need a DOM user gesture, which a MainButton press is not.
6. The SecondaryButton is never «Скасувати» where Back already cancels. Native popups (`showPopup`) only for short
   destructive yes/no («Скасувати замовлення №1044?»). Money confirmations are in-app sheets with a big number.
7. `lockOrientation('portrait')` while a basket is open and during the count; unlock on exit.
8. MainButton colours: `setParams({color: primary, text_color: on-primary})` per theme (§5). SecondaryButton
   `setParams({color: card, text_color: ink})`, `position: 'top'`.
9. `requestWriteAccess` is asked only from the Today tag «Ранкове повідомлення від бота — увімкнути?», never at launch.

segment, a store, a filter knot, a chip; `impactOccurred('light')` — a coat into the basket, a stepper ±1, a tag verb;

`notificationOccurred('error')` — refused by the server, a validation error. Never on scroll, keystrokes or screen entry.
In the demo each haptic is a 160 ms ring pulse on the phone frame in the haptic's semantic colour plus
`navigator.vibrate` (10 / 20 / 30 ms) in try/catch.

## 5. Tokens

All values are also in `spec/tokens.json` (keys identical to the names below; CSS custom property = `--<name>`).
**Every colour used anywhere in the canvas or the demo comes from this section.** Light values live on bare `:root`;
dark values under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-theme="light"]) {…} }` and again under
`:root[data-theme="dark"] {…}`, each with `color-scheme: dark`. `body` has `background: var(--paper)`.

### 5.1 The idea: greyboard, ink and one thread
A hang tag is grey card stock printed in black ink and tied on with a coloured thread. The neutrals are tinted toward
olive (hue ≈ 70–80°) so they read as card, never as cream. Ink is a green-black. There is **one accent, `thread`**, an
acid lime like the zip-tie on a fashion tag, and it has one fixed meaning: **"your hand is on it right now"** — the chosen
size, the primary button, the pay buttons, the capture pill, the active tab, an exact drawer match. It never decorates.

**The flip rule.** Lime on white is 1.33:1, so lime never sits on a light surface. In the **light** theme, thread is
printed only **on ink**: an ink button with a lime label, an ink cell with a lime letter. In the **dark** theme, thread
becomes the **surface**: a lime button with an ink label, a lime cell with an ink letter. Tokens `primary/on-primary` and
`chosen/on-chosen` encode the flip, so components never branch on theme.

**The dark "ink tag" stays dark** (owner's answer of 2026-10-21, BR:3458): in dark it is `inverse #262B1E`, light text,
a 1 px `inverse-edge #7E8570` border at ≥ 3:1 against the card and the page, and its title in `thread`.

**Owner check on the accent [Q-2].** The owner approved a deep green on 2026-10-11; lime is a deliberate departure. The
Palette board shows the same screen twice, identical except `thread #C6EF3E` vs `thread-alt #B9C86E` (an olive in A's
loden range, same flip rule). The demo's presenter has a toggle "Thread: Lime | Olive" that swaps only `--thread`,
`--on-thread`, `--primary`/`--on-primary` and `--chosen`/`--on-chosen` (dark: primary = thread-alt; light: on-primary =
thread-alt).

### 5.5 Type
**One Google Fonts link (the only network resource):**
```
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fira+Sans+Extra+Condensed:wght@700;800&family=Fira+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap
```
- **Display — Fira Sans Extra Condensed 700/800.** A narrow, sturdy grotesk that reads like a printed garment label;
  fits «XS S M L XL XXL» across 358 px at 28 px and a five-figure sum with kopecks at 56 px. Used for screen titles, money
  ≥ 28 px, size letters, model names on hero tags, keypad digits.
- **Text — Fira Sans 400/500/600/700**, the same superfamily at normal width, for everything else in the canvas and the
  demo. List money ≤ 20 px uses Fira Sans 600 tnum (never the condensed face below 22 px).
- **Shipped app:** only the display face ships (800, latin + cyrillic woff2, `font-display: optional` on the till); body
  falls back to the system stack (0 KB).
- Fallback stacks: display `"Fira Sans Extra Condensed", "Fira Sans Condensed", "Arial Narrow", system-ui, -apple-system,
  "Segoe UI", sans-serif`; text `"Fira Sans", system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto,
  "Noto Sans", sans-serif`.
- Verified 2026-10-22 on the served files: Cyrillic, Cyrillic-ext and Latin subsets served; `tnum` and `locl` present;
  і ї є ґ ’ ₴ № « » − present; standard Ukrainian letterforms under `lang="uk"`; tabular digits equal width in Chromium.
  Rejected: Sofia Sans (Bulgarian forms, no ₴), Arsenal (no tnum), Inter / Space Grotesk / Roboto (banned as main face).
- `lang="en"` on English boards and the demo in EN; `lang="uk"` on Ukrainian strings (the demo sets `lang` on `<html>`
  with the language toggle).

## 7. Voice and copy

### 7.1 Rules
1. **«ви», short shop words, verb first.** «Закрити касу», «Зібрати й відправити» — never «Здійснити закриття». English:
   plain imperative, sentence case, no "please".
2. **The number before the words:** «4 800 грн · винна «Центру»»; "₴4,800 · owes Tsentr".
3. **Say where the money is,** never an accounting term («у перевізника», not «дебіторська заборгованість»).
4. **Every write states its fate:** recorded, kept on this phone, or not recorded. Never «Спробуйте ще раз» for a write
   («Спробувати ще раз» exists only for re-checking access).
5. **Nobody is blamed.** A shortage is «Нестача», warning colour, no "by whose fault".
6. **Rules are invisible:** never «Немає права». What someone may not do is not there.
7. **One line each:** what happened, then what to do. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no «чек» (Birka records sales; it
   does not issue fiscal receipts).
8. **Product words win:** «Покупець» at the till and in debts; «Клієнт забрав» as the order step; «Внесок»; «Каса»;
   «Борги»; «Брак»; «ТТН». English uses the glossary below and keeps proper names transliterated (Tsentr, Rynok, Loran).
9. **Gender only when the text knows the person** (a typed name): «Наталя порахувала»; otherwise neutral «Порахували»,
   «Винні». Prepared replies to customers use the vocative («Марино, …») and are always drafts she sends herself.
10. Typography: «ялинки» quotes in Ukrainian, “curly” quotes in English, the apostrophe ’ (U+2019), the dash — with
    spaces, U+00A0 inside numbers and before «грн», U+2212 for minus, «№» before order numbers in Ukrainian and «#» in
    English.
