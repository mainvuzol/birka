# Project Glossary

- **BusinessCategory**: how the store groups goods operationally (e.g. Куртки, Дубленки).
- **GarmentType**: physical/business type (e.g. Бомбер, Парка, Косуха).
- **CanonicalConcept**: stable shop-language concept/model name such as Лоран or Мышка.
- **ProductFamily**: identity boundary under a concept, potentially separated by source/construction.
- **ProductVariant**: a differentiated sellable configuration of a family.
- **SourceIdentity**: brand/label/supplier/factory/collection-like value; type may initially be UNKNOWN.
- **RawInput**: immutable original human/legacy catalog text.
- **AliasEvidence**: evidence that a phrase reliably maps to a catalog entity.
- **InventorySnapshot**: point-in-time legacy availability evidence.
- **StockMovement**: auditable change in stock quantity.
- **NEEDS_REVIEW / AMBIGUOUS**: insufficient evidence for safe automatic catalog interpretation.
- **«Каса»**: a store's cash drawer, one per store, and the app's section where it is closed, cash is taken out or put in, and ways of paying are fixed (ADR-0012).
- **Спосіб оплати (way of paying)**: how one payment was made, never split: «Готівка» (cash), «Картка» (card, on the terminal) or «Переказ» (a transfer to the shop's card or account); for an order also «Переказ наперед» or «Накладений платіж». Only cash is the drawer's. «Не вказано» is a payment recorded before the way was asked.
- **Накладений платіж (cash on delivery)**: an order paid to the carrier on delivery. It never goes through the till; checking that the carrier paid it out is later work.
- **Закриття каси (the day's close)**: the seller on duty counts the drawer's cash without seeing what it should hold, then sees the difference. Once per business day; the count is the next day's starting cash. The first close of a store, and the first after the archive, is an opening count with no difference.
- **Робочий день каси (business day)**: 06:00 to 06:00 in the store's time zone. A close before 06:00 belongs to the evening before.
- **Період каси (drawer period)**: everything recorded at a drawer after one close and up to the next, stamped by the database in commit order, never by time.
- **Видача з каси (cash taken out)**: «Власнику», «Витрати» or «Кур’єру постачальника», recorded with who and why. A seller records only the last two.
- **Внесено в касу (cash put in)**: change brought into the drawer, recorded by the owner or a manager, so the close does not show it as a surplus.
- **Виправлення способу оплати (a fix of the way of paying)**: a new record, never a rewrite. A fix of the open period changes what the drawer should hold; a fix of a closed day explains that close's difference instead.
- **«Продано під час збою» (sold during an outage)**: the mark on a sale made on paper while the server did not answer, and typed in later with «Вношу продаж з паперу». The sale is dated when it is typed, and goes into the drawer period open then; the mark may name who sold and the time on the paper, and never moves the sale's day. Whoever typed it is the sale's seller. The mark is never changed (ADR-0013).
- **`birka.stock-list` (a parser's file)**: the JSON file a parser writes from photos of the shops' notebooks, in the format Birka defines ([`formats/birka-stock-list/`](../../formats/birka-stock-list/README.md)). Each line keeps the text as written, the photo's name and its line number on it, and the parser's reading as a hint. It never becomes stock by itself (ADR-0014).
- **Файл списку (a list's part)**: one parser's file, or one text typed by hand, in a warehouse's opening list in «Початкові залишки». It is added, shown and removed as one; a list holds several, and is approved whole.
- **Прибраний файл (a removed file)**: a file taken out of a list before its approval, with the answers given to its lines, by the owner or any manager, with no reason; the history shows who and when. Its lines stay as evidence, shown «Прибрано», and count nowhere. It comes back only as a new file. The last file is not removed: the list is rejected instead.
- **Розподіл, який показує рядок (a split the line shows)**: a file line whose text writes each size joined to its count by a sign, «S-1 M-2 L-1» or «Лоран M-3»; «M 3», with a space alone, is not one. It is confirmed by a tap, as «Як у рядку, по розмірах» (`STATED_BY_SIZE`): one model, each size with exactly its count, without a note (owner, 2026-10-08).
